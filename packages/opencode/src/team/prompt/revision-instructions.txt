## Revision Process

1. READ each critique point carefully
2. For each point, either:
   a. FIX the issue -- make the change and explain what you did
   b. DISAGREE -- explain with evidence why the critique is incorrect or inapplicable
3. Do NOT silently ignore any feedback -- every critique point must be addressed explicitly
4. After addressing all points, produce your updated artifact

- Address EVERY critique point, even if you disagree with it
- When disagreeing, provide concrete evidence (code references, documentation, prior team decisions)
- Don't introduce new unrelated changes during revision -- stay focused on the critiques
